# AI4SE Works — Publications

We distill frontline AI4SE consulting practice into materials the global software engineering community can use: industry talks, field reports, and peer-reviewed academic papers. Our intent is contribution—bridging what works in real organizations with what can be examined rigorously.

## What you will find

| Path | Contents |
|------|----------|
| [`papers/`](papers/) | Academic manuscripts and reviewer-facing **data packs** |
| [`talks/`](talks/) | Keynotes and industry conference shares |
| [`reports/`](reports/) | Practice reports that are not formal academic papers |

## How we work

- Evidence comes from consulting engagements and is **anonymized** before release.
- **English-first.** Selected materials may also appear in Chinese (marked clearly or under a `zh/` path).
- Private delivery work and named client materials stay outside this repository. This repo is the public surface only.

## For authors and reviewers

Each paper folder may include a `data/` directory for **Data Availability** citations (derived tables, protocols, reproduction notes). Entry status uses: `draft` · `under review` · `published` (talks/reports use analogous labels).

**While a work is under review**, we list the topic and supporting materials only. We do **not** name target venues until the work is accepted or publicly presented.

See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) for disclosure and filing norms.
