# AI4SE Works — Artifacts

We distill frontline AI4SE consulting practice into materials the global software engineering community can use: industry talks, field reports, and academic papers. Our intent is contribution—bridging what works in real organizations with what can be examined rigorously.

## What you will find

| Path | Contents |
|------|----------|
| [`manuscripts/`](manuscripts/) | Academic **manuscripts** under preparation or review, plus reviewer-facing **data packs** |
| [`papers/`](papers/) | **Accepted or published** academic papers (moved here after acceptance) |
| [`talks/`](talks/) | Keynotes and industry conference shares |
| [`reports/`](reports/) | Practice reports that are not formal academic papers |

## How we work

- Evidence comes from consulting engagements and is **anonymized** before release.
- **English-first.** Selected materials may also appear in Chinese (marked clearly or under a `zh/` path).
- Private delivery work and named client materials stay outside this repository. This repo is the public surface only.

## Lifecycle (academic work)

1. **Manuscript stage** — place materials under `manuscripts/<slug>/` (optional `data/` for Data Availability citations).
2. **After acceptance / publication** — move the entry to `papers/<slug>/` and add venue, DOI, or proceedings links.

**While a work is under review**, we list the topic and supporting materials only. We do **not** name target venues until the work is accepted or publicly presented.

See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) for disclosure and filing norms.
