# Stage-gated AI4SE transformation path (Org-A SSOT)

**Paper B use:** §III (Stage-Gated Path) and §IV gate/scale-entry framing for Org-A.  
**Client label:** Org-A only — no legal names, product brands, or program identifiers.

---

## Three-stage path (canonical English)

The Org-A engagement follows a **stage-gated transformation path** with three phases. Each phase has explicit goals, deliverables, and exit criteria. Phases are **dependency-linked**: the prior phase’s outputs are inputs to the next; **calendar dates do not override unmet exit criteria**.

### Stage 1 — Explore (pilot exploration)

| Dimension | Org-A fact |
|---|---|
| **Goal** | Validate that the AI4SE method is feasible in a real delivery context; run the method **end-to-end** |
| **Duration** | ~**6 weeks** (approximately one calendar month: early July → early August 2026) |
| **Key actions** | Kickoff diagnosis and scenario lock; four workshop weeks on twelve opportunity themes; co-create initial Playbook and executable-asset scaffold; use a **validation vehicle** (pilot change request) to prove the method in use |
| **Deliverables** | Initial Playbook (v1 / v0.5→closeout draft); opportunity-point Spec/BP assets; pilot case evidence |
| **Exit criterion** | **End-to-end run-through** — method chain exercised, not “all business features shipped” |

### Stage 2 — Consolidation (pilot hardening / seed capability)

| Dimension | Org-A fact |
|---|---|
| **Goal** | Upgrade “can run once” to “can replicate”: **benchmark team + seed coaches** |
| **Duration** | ~**1–2 months** (early August → end September 2026, indicative) |
| **Key actions** | Iterate Playbook (remove “reads right but doesn’t run” clauses); harden toolset / executable assets (stub → ready on main chain); clarify role ownership; internal coach-the-coach drills |
| **Deliverables** | Standardized Playbook v2; toolset; seed coaches |
| **Exit criterion** | **Team can teach** — each member can explain and transfer the method (“everyone can teach”) |

### Stage 3 — Scale (conditional organization-wide diffusion)

| Dimension | Org-A fact |
|---|---|
| **Goal** | **Quality-consistent diffusion**; new teams operate independently with guardrails |
| **Entry conditions** | Stage 2 exit met; role owners named; Harness starter kit available for sibling teams; sponsor rhythm and resources reserved — **not automatic after pilot success** |
| **Key actions** | Seed-coach embedded enablement; wave-based rollout; practice review for consistency |
| **Deliverables** | Rollout handbook; evidence of new-team self-run validation |
| **Exit criterion** | **New team self-run** — a fresh team completes a cycle without consultant-led delivery |

**Org-A posture after phase-one closeout:** Stage 1 complete → **consolidation entered**; Stage 3 treated as **conditional entry** (calendar target ~October 2026 is subordinate to Stage 2 gate).

---

## Anti-pattern (explicit in paper)

**Pilot success ≠ scale entry.** A successful explore pilot proves **feasibility** and produces first-generation method assets; it does **not** by itself satisfy scale-entry conditions (replicability, seed coaches, executable assets beyond scaffold, role/Harness prerequisites).

---

## Scale-feasibility argument (required in paper)

1. **Path designed** — stages, deliverables, and management gates defined before claiming scale readiness  
2. **Explore validated end-to-end** — Playbook v1 + MVP/validation vehicle exercised the method chain under real constraints  
3. **Scale conditionally feasible** — pilot succeeded but **scale entry not met**; consolidation is the prerequisite; feasibility judgment rests on asset readiness and team self-solve capability, not on fictional org-wide ROI
