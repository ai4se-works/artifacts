## Why measurement mattered

Stakeholders repeatedly asked for **productivity proof** of the AI4SE change: how much “faster / more throughput” under the new method, and whether organizational capability was actually moving. The consulting response was a **dual-lens** reporting scheme—capability maturity before/after plus bounded productivity *signals*—rather than a single multiplier slogan.

## Pilot posture (facts usable in paper)

- Scope: method enablement and evidence collection; delivery work was a **validation vehicle**, not the primary acceptance criterion for the phase.
- Duration: ~6 weeks from kickoff through executive readout of phase one.
- Delivery model: multi-team agile; PBIs in shared requirements management platform; Dev/BA/QA split.
- Quality: treated as a standing red line; efficiency asks under non-degrading quality; retained client quality gates (e.g., defect escape / density)—not re-analyzed in this paper.
- Outputs relevant to this paper: maturity re-assessment; facilitated effectiveness workshop under fixed Product Backlog / team / iteration timebox; signal-type boundaries stated to management.
- Explicit non-claim: phase success ≠ scale-ready; expert-estimate workshop results ≠ platform-measured ROI.

## Post-explore posture (Paper B)

After phase-one closeout, Org-A entered **consolidation** (method hardening / seed capability), with scale treated as **conditional entry**, not an automatic next calendar step.
